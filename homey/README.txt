KI Energi keeps your electricity bill down without you having to think about it.

It knows the Norwegian grid tariffs, capacity steps, electricity subsidy and Norgespris, fetches spot prices itself, and shows what your power actually costs right now and so far this month.

On top of that it controls heating, the water heater and EV charging so you stay below your capacity step, heats when power is cheap, lowers the temperature when nobody is home, and has the house warm again when you return.

It starts in shadow mode: it shows what it would have done, but controls nothing until you turn shadow mode off.
